# Dubai Moving Cost Calculator (2026)

A free, interactive moving-cost estimator for Dubai — pick your home size, extra services and moving day, and get an instant AED price range based on real 2026 market rates.

**Try it live:** https://museai5610-cmd.github.io/dubai-moving-cost-calculator/

## Why this exists

Moving quotes in Dubai vary wildly. This tool gives you an honest ballpark before you call anyone:
- Studio: AED 600 – 900
- 1-bedroom: AED 800 – 1,200
- 2-bedroom: AED 1,200 – 2,000
- 3-bedroom: AED 1,800 – 2,800
- Villa: AED 2,500 – 4,500
- Office: AED 2,000 – 6,000

Ranges compiled from 25 years of written estimates. Read the full breakdown in the [2026 Dubai moving cost guide](https://luckygmovers.com/moving-cost-dubai/).
